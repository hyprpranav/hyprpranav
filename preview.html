🌿 FEATURED ENGINEERING PROJECTS // IoT ARCHITECTURES

🌲 01. FloraSense (Forest Biosphere IoT)

Deep forest canopy environmental sensing station. Logs forest humidity, soil moisture gradient, river stream turbidity, and acoustic wildlife bio-telemetry over ultra-low-power LoRa mesh nodes powered by energy-harvesting solar capacitors.

Hardware: ESP32-S3, LoRa SX1262, SHT31, Solar MPPT Harvester

Software: MicroPython, MQTT, InfluxDB, Leaflet Eco-Map Visualizer

🔗 hyprpranav/florasense-canopy-iot

🌍 02. GroundGuard

Autonomous geotechnical & seismic early-warning sensor station. Continuous multi-axis vibration analysis, soil liquefaction detection, and tilt telemetry capable of triggering acoustic alarms and broadcast alerts over emergency LoRaWAN mesh networks.

Hardware: ESP32-S3, ADXL355 High-Res Accelerometer, LoRa SX1262

Software: MicroPython, MQTT, Node-RED, GeoJSON Mapping API

🔗 hyprpranav/groundguard-early-warning

🛡️ 03. NeoGuard

Smart perimeter surveillance and threat neutralization node. Integrates dual PIR and ultrasonic sensor fusion with edge vision to detect intrusions and broadcast encrypted instant telegram/email telematics with snapshot verification.

Hardware: Raspberry Pi Zero 2W, ESP32-CAM, Ultrasonic Arrays, GSM/GPRS

Software: OpenCV Edge Inference, C++, WebSockets, Telegram Bot API

🔗 hyprpranav/neoguard-security-system

💡 04. NeoLuma

Intelligent municipal adaptive street-lighting grid. Dynamically modulates luminance based on vehicular traffic density, pedestrian presence, and ambient lux levels, delivering up to 42% energy reduction.

Hardware: ATmega328P / ESP8266, LDR Lux Sensor, Microwave Doppler Radar

Software: Embedded C, MQTT Broker, React Admin Portal, Firebase

🔗 hyprpranav/neoluma-smart-lighting

🩺 05. Beltiva

Ergonomic smart wearable assistive belt engineered for real-time lumbar posture tracking, geriatric fall impact detection, and respiratory rhythm monitoring with BLE transmission to mobile apps.

Hardware: Nordic nRF52840, MPU-6050 6-DOF IMU, Flexible Strain Sensor

Software: Zephyr RTOS, BLE GATT Server, Flutter Companion App

🔗 hyprpranav/beltiva-smart-wearable

🚗 06. AutoSense

Intelligent vehicular blind-spot radar and collision avoidance auxiliary computer. Combines multi-directional millimeter-wave / ultrasonic proximity sensors with vehicle speed telemetry.

Hardware: STM32 Nucleo, 24GHz mmWave Radar, CAN Transceiver, OLED HUD

Software: Embedded C++, FreeRTOS, OBD-II Protocol Interface

🔗 hyprpranav/autosense-vehicular-telematics

🚆 07. Smart Railway Safety System

Automated railway track crack detection, obstruction mitigation, and unmanned level-crossing safety module. Utilizes laser triangulation, IR arrays, and acoustic emission transducers on inspection trolleys.

Hardware: Arduino Mega / ESP32, Laser Profiler, GPS NEO-6M, GSM SIM800L

Software: Embedded C, SMS & Cloud Webhook Telemetry, Leaflet GIS Dashboard

🔗 hyprpranav/smart-railway-safety-system
